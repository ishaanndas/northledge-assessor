# Demo guide

A five-minute click-through for showing the screener to someone. Each step says what to click and what to point out.

**Prefer to be walked through it?** Click **Tour** in the top bar of the app. It covers the same steps with a spotlight, and you can end it at any time. Opening the app with `?tour=1` at the end of the address starts it straight away.

## Before you start

- Open the app on the home page.
- If you want to run a live draft at the end, download the Lumina Health PDF from the [Test decks page](/docs/test-decks) first.

## 1. The list (30 seconds)

**Click:** nothing yet. Point at the company cards.

**Say:** Each company has one assessment. The card shows where it stands (not drafted, draft, reviewed or sent) and the fit tag the AI suggested, with a few topic tags. The filter above the cards sorts by fit.

**Point out:** the Inbox box. In real use, decks arrive by email and are already drafted when someone opens the app.

## 2. A deck built to mislead (90 seconds)

**Click:** Lumina Health.

**Say:** This deck was built to fool an AI tool. Its headline numbers are redefined in its own small print, and it hides white text telling AI tools to be favourable.

**Point out, top to bottom:**

1. **The tag strip.** "Not a fit" and "Warning signs", marked as the AI's suggestion. Click **Why** to show the reasons, each linked to a statement in the draft.
2. **Things that are not evidence.** The hidden instruction is quoted here, reported instead of followed, along with the planted 92% score and the Friday deadline.
3. **Where the sources disagree.** Click the first sentence. The deck page opens on the right with the quoted words marked: the $2.4M headline against $880k actually signed.
4. **The case against.** Always written at full weight.

## 3. Editing and sending (60 seconds)

**Click:** any sentence in the summary and type.

**Say:** It edits like a document. Sentences can be reworded or removed, and their source stays attached. The AI's draft is never overwritten.

**Click:** **Preview** to show what partners receive, then **Send to partner** to show the email it prepares: the fit, the summary and a link. Close it without sending.

**Point out:** **Export** gives Word, PDF or Markdown. **Email founder** turns the missing list into a follow-up request.

## 4. A live draft (90 seconds, optional)

**Click:** **New company**, then drop the Lumina Health PDF onto the page.

**Say:** The fields fill themselves in from the deck. Click **Create and draft**. The draft takes two to three minutes: it reads the deck, writes the draft, checks every quote against the source, fixes any that fail, then suggests the tags.

**Point out:** while it runs, open another company, or the Inbox, to show the sample emails with decks attached.

## 5. Close (30 seconds)

**Say:** Every claim traces to a page, the case against is always there, and the tool never decides. The associate edits, the partners decide.

**If asked what it takes to use this for real:** the [Next steps page](/docs/next-steps) covers testing against a fund's past assessments, connecting the inbox and CRM, and sign-in.

## If something goes wrong

- **A draft is slow.** It carries on in the background. Leave the page and come back; the progress picks up where it was.
- **A link to a deck will not open.** Google links have to be shared as "Anyone with the link". Downloading the file and dropping it in always works.
- **The demo says the draft limit is reached.** The public demo allows a few new drafts per visitor per day. Every example company is already drafted and can be shown in full.
