Kestrel

AI copilot for independent insurance adjusters

The problem

There are 58,000 independent adjusters in the US handling property claims for carriers. A single hail claim takes 3.5 hours of documentation: photos, measurements, line-item estimates in Xactimate, carrier-specific narratives. Adjusters are paid per claim, so documentation time is unpaid time.

2

Product

Kestrel listens during the inspection, tags photos as they are taken, drafts the Xactimate line items and writes the carrier narrative in the carrier's format. The adjuster reviews and submits. Average documentation time drops from 3.5 hours to 50 minutes in our pilots.
