Northwind Battery Analytics

Warranty-grade health reports for second-life EV battery packs

Used EV batteries are sold blind

About 400,000 EV packs will leave first-life vehicles in North America in 2027. Buyers of second-life packs (stationary storage integrators, remanufacturers, recyclers) price them on age and mileage because nobody can certify remaining capacity at scale.

A pack worth $4,200 as storage is scrapped for $900 of materials when its health is unknown.

2

What Northwind does

A 40-minute bench test using our fixture plus a model trained on 11,000 pack cycles produces a health certificate: remaining capacity, internal resistance drift, cell imbalance and a 24-month degradation forecast. The certificate is what the buyer underwrites against.

  • Hardware fixture leased to dismantlers and auction yards
  • Certificate priced per pack
  • Buyers subscribe to the certified inventory feed

Competition

OEM diagnostics (Tesla, GM) read packs from their own vehicles only and do not issue transferable certificates. ReJoule and Voltaiq sell lab-grade analyzers at $40k+ to labs, not to the yards where packs change hands. Northwind is the only certificate that a buyer can underwrite against at the point of sale.
