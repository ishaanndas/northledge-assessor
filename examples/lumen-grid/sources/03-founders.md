# Founder bios (provided by the company)

Priya Raman, co-founder and CEO. Eight years at Pacific Gas & Electric, 2016 to 2024. Senior engineer in distribution planning, then manager of the distribution interconnection group from 2021, overseeing a team of 14 engineers and analysts. BS and MS in electrical engineering, UC Davis. Left PG&E in September 2024 to start Lumen Grid.

Daniel Okafor, co-founder and CTO. Forward-deployed engineer at Palantir from 2017 to 2022, where he led utility and energy deployments. Staff engineer at Camus Energy from 2022 to 2024, building grid data integrations for cooperative utilities. BS in computer science, Georgia Tech.

Priya and Daniel met in 2023 when Camus Energy integrated with PG&E's interconnection systems. They have worked together full-time since October 2024.
