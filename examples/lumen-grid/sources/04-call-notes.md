# First call notes — Lumen Grid, 18 June 2026 (associate: M. Chen, 45 minutes with Priya and Daniel)

Priya walked through the queue problem with specifics from her PG&E time. Credible, unusually detailed on tariff mechanics. Daniel did the product demo; the tariff rule engine is the differentiated piece and took them nine months to build.

Revenue. Confirmed $38k MRR. Of the six paying utilities, four are on annual contracts and two (Pinecrest and Harlan County) are month-to-month while they finish procurement approval. Cascade PUD is the largest at $110k per year and is 24% of MRR.

Pilots. Great Lakes Energy pilot started in April, Southern Tier in May. Both are paid at $15k for six months. Conversion criteria are written into the pilot agreements. Priya said she would put the odds of both converting at "better than even" but would not commit to more than that.

Churn. No logo churn to date. One customer (Blue Mesa) went quiet for two months during a staff change and has since re-engaged.

Competition. Asked about PowerClerk (Clean Power Research) which is widely used. Priya's view: PowerClerk handles application intake well but does not model study workflow or tariff completeness rules; several Lumen customers still use PowerClerk for intake and Lumen for everything after. She acknowledged PowerClerk could extend into their space. GridUnity is the other named competitor; focused on transmission-level work and larger IOUs.

Sales. The cooperative and muni motion is referral-driven through state cooperative associations. IOU sales require a procurement process and a security review; the SOC 2 Type I was done specifically to unblock Great Lakes.

Hiring. Seven people. Two engineers are contractors in Portugal they intend to convert to full-time after the raise.

Round. $2.5M on a $14M post-money SAFE. They have $600k soft-circled from an energy angel syndicate. Runway at current burn is 11 months without the raise.

Follow-ups: reference calls with Cascade PUD and Three Rivers; copy of the pilot agreements; cohort data behind the 118% NRR figure.
