Lumen Grid: Interconnection, in order. Software that helps utility interconnection teams clear their queue faster, with less paperwork and fewer errors.

How it works. Applicants submit through a branded portal. Lumen Grid validates every application against your tariff rules before an engineer ever sees it, assigns study milestones, tracks deadlines under FERC Order 2023 and state timelines, and generates the quarterly queue report your commission expects.

Built by people who ran interconnection. Our founding team ran a distribution interconnection group at a major California utility and has processed more than 12,000 applications first-hand.

Customers. Cascade PUD cut its average application review time from 31 days to 9 days in the first quarter on Lumen Grid. Three Rivers Electric Cooperative eliminated its backlog of 240 pending applications within five months.
