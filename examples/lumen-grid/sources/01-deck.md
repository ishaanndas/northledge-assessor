# Lumen Grid — Seed Deck (June 2026)

## Slide 1: Interconnection is the bottleneck of the energy transition
Over 2,600 GW of generation and storage projects are waiting in US interconnection queues. The median project now waits almost five years for a utility study. Utilities run these studies with spreadsheets, email and a handful of overworked engineers.

## Slide 2: What we built
Lumen Grid is workflow software for utility interconnection teams. It ingests applications, tracks study milestones, runs completeness checks against each utility's tariff, and produces the regulator-facing queue reports automatically. Engineers spend their time on power-flow analysis instead of chasing paperwork.

## Slide 3: Traction
Six utilities are paying customers today, representing $38,000 in monthly recurring revenue. Two additional investor-owned utilities are in paid pilots that convert to annual contracts in Q4 2026 if they hit agreed milestones. Net revenue retention across the six paying accounts is 118% on a trailing-twelve-month basis.

## Slide 4: Customers
Paying: Cascade Public Utility District, Three Rivers Electric Cooperative, Northfield Municipal Light, Blue Mesa Cooperative, Pinecrest Utilities, Harlan County Electric. Piloting: Great Lakes Energy (IOU, 1.1M meters) and Southern Tier Power (IOU, 640k meters).

## Slide 5: Business model
Annual SaaS subscription priced by meter count and queue volume. Current contracts range from $42,000 to $110,000 per year. Gross margin is 81%. Average sales cycle for cooperatives and munis has been 4 months; the two IOU pilots took 9 months to sign.

## Slide 6: Market
There are roughly 3,000 electric utilities in the United States. We count 410 that process more than 50 interconnection applications per year, which is our serviceable market. At our current average contract value this is a $29M annual opportunity in the US before expanding into transmission-level studies and Canada.
