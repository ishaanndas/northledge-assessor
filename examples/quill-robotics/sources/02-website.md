# quillrobotics.com (captured 20 May 2026)

Quill Robotics. Robots that sew.

Our cells handle the seams that make up 70% of sewing time on a basic garment: side seams, hems, and pocket attachment. Vision-guided fabric handling means no fixtures, no special fabric treatment, and changeovers in under ten minutes.

Deployed with contract manufacturers in North Carolina, Texas and Monterrey.

Robotics-as-a-service. No capital expenditure. One monthly fee covers the cell, software updates and maintenance.

Leadership. Elena Marsh, CEO: former Tesla perception engineer and Gap Inc. sourcing lead. Raj Venkat, CTO: Carnegie Mellon Robotics Institute.
