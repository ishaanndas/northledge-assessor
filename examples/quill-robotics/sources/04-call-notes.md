# First call notes — Quill Robotics, 27 May 2026 (associate: M. Chen, 50 minutes with Elena Marsh and Raj Venkat)

Strong demo video. Raj is clearly the technical engine; the deformable-handling approach is genuinely hard and his CMU work is real and well cited.

Revenue. I asked Elena to walk through the $1.4M ARR. Contracted ARR today is $410k: 14 cells at roughly $2,400 per cell per month (early customers got a discount from the $4,200 list price). The $1.4M figure is contracted ARR plus the annualized value of signed LOIs that have not yet converted to orders. Elena agreed the deck should say "contracted plus committed" and said she would update it.

Customers. Two contract manufacturers (North Carolina and Texas) and one brand facility in Monterrey. The Monterrey facility has 8 of the 14 cells and is 55% of contracted revenue.

Team background. Asked Elena about the Tesla role. She was a contractor on the Optimus perception team for seven months in 2022 through an agency, working on data labeling pipelines. She did not lead the team. She was open about this when asked directly but the deck and bio overstate it. Raj's background checks out.

Market. The $48B figure on slide 4 is McKinsey's number for warehouse and industrial automation broadly. Elena acknowledged there is no good market-size figure for sewing automation specifically. Her bottom-up estimate: 8,000 apparel factories in the Americas with more than 50 operators, each a candidate for 5 to 20 cells.

Unit economics. Hardware cost per cell $31k confirmed. At the discounted $2,400 rate, hardware payback is 13 months, not 8. Field maintenance has been done by Raj personally flying to sites; they have no service team yet.

Reliability. Cells average 71% uptime at the Monterrey site because of fabric variation on new SKUs. The two US sites, which run fewer SKUs, are above 90%.

Round. $4M at $18M post. No lead yet. Two angels committed $350k total.

Follow-ups: cell-level uptime logs, customer contracts and LOIs, reference call with the Monterrey plant manager, confirmation of Elena's Tesla engagement dates.
