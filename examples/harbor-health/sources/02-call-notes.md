# First call notes — Harbor Health, 3 July 2026 (associate: M. Chen, 25 minutes with founder Tom Alvarez; co-founder did not join)

Tom was referred by a portfolio founder. He described Harbor as "an operating system for home health" and spent most of the call on the size of the home health market and the staffing crisis.

Asked how many agencies use the product. Tom said "a few pilots" and that he would rather not share numbers until the round is further along. Asked whether any pilot is paying; he said one has "committed to pay" starting in September.

Asked about the team. Tom is a former home health agency operator (ran a three-branch agency in Arizona, sold it in 2023). His co-founder is "a senior ML engineer" he met through a friend; Tom did not give a name and said they are "finalizing the equity split."

Asked about the OASIS documentation feature on the website. Tom said it is "in development" and that the pilots are using the scheduling module only.

Raising $3M. No lead. Tom said two other funds are "interested."

Tom was personable and clearly knows home health operations. He was reluctant on every quantitative question. Suggested follow-up: ask for a deck, pilot names, co-founder background and a product demo before spending more time.
