Harbor Health. The AI care coordination layer for home health agencies.

Home health agencies lose hours every day to scheduling calls, visit documentation and payer paperwork. Harbor automates the coordination work so clinicians can focus on patients.

Smart scheduling. Harbor matches visits to clinicians by skill, location and authorization status, and reschedules automatically when a visit falls through.

Documentation that writes itself. Clinicians speak their visit notes; Harbor drafts OASIS-compliant documentation for review and signature.

Payer-ready. Harbor tracks authorizations and flags visits at risk of denial before they happen.
