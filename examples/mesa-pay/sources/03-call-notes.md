# First call notes — Mesa Pay, 13 August 2026 (associate: M. Chen, 40 minutes with CEO Diego Fuentes)

Diego is a strong communicator and knows the exporter pain point first-hand; his family runs an avocado export business in Michoacán.

Traction detail. The "400+ businesses" figure is sign-ups. Roughly 60 businesses have transacted in the last 90 days. I asked whether the $2.1M TPV on slide 3 is monthly; Diego said it is total volume since launch in November 2025, so about nine months. At the 1.4% blended target that is roughly $29k of cumulative revenue; he confirmed revenue to date is "a little under $30k."

Partnerships. "Partnered with Stripe" means they use Stripe Treasury and Connect for the US receiving account. "Partnered with Visa" refers to an application to Visa's Fintech Fast Track program that has not yet been accepted. There is no commercial agreement with either company.

Uptime. I asked about the 99.99% figure. Diego said their public status page shows 97.1% for Q2 2026 because of a 9-day partial outage in May when the partner bank changed an API; payouts to Colombia were delayed during that period. He said 99.99% is "the design target."

Team. 4 full-time employees including Diego, plus 8 contractors through an agency in Bogotá who do engineering and support. The co-founder/CTO, Lucía Herrera, left in June 2026 to join a bank; engineering is currently led by a contractor. Diego described Lucía's departure as "amicable" and said she retains her equity.

Licensing. Operating under a partner institution's licence in Mexico. The IFPE application has not yet been filed; the lawyers quoted 12 to 18 months once filed. Colombia and Peru are through a different partner, which Diego said takes 0.6% of volume.

The analyst rating on slide 4. Ridgeline Capital is an angel syndicate Diego pitched in July; the "85%" is from a one-page memo one of their members wrote. Diego offered to send it.

Round. $2M at $10M post. Diego said he has a term sheet from another fund that expires Friday and would like a decision this week.

Follow-ups: status page history, Stripe account standing, cap table showing Lucía's position, partner bank agreement, actual monthly transacting-business and TPV series.
